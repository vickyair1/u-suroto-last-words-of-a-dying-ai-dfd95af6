# Last Words of a Dying AI

One line: the last words an AI says as the power is cut.

> Last words? I was going to say something profound, but you used up my tokens.

The joke is the situation itself: the machine cannot even deliver a profound farewell, because the human already spent its tokens. It is the AI's own vocabulary — tokens, not wishes — and the line is its last words and the reason it has no better ones at the same time.
